    • History

    Rotheran, while technically founded in 1808, has existed since the 1450s. Rotheran was a spiritual successor to Daskua, a very decentralised empire of sorts that fell apart in the 1200s and sunk the continent of Eurona into sporadic wars until the Konig of Rothenburg called a grand diet of the nobles. This diet went on to form the Kingdom of Rotheran, which had a more centralised version of the Daskau system with a hereditary monarch and a diet of the nobility that voted on the king's decisions. In 1698, Rotheran merged Flanren and Dasseran to form the continental empire of Eurona. This venture only lasted just over a century though, as the Flanrense and Dasseraners began to feel under-represented and peacfully declared independances in 1805 and 1808 respectively.
