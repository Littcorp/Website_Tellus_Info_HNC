History

Rotheran, while technically founded in 1808, has existed since the 1450s. Rotheran was a spiritual successor to Daskau, a very decentralised empire of sorts that fell apart in the 1200s and sunk the continent of Eurona into sporadic wars until the Konig of Rothenburg called a grand diet of the nobles. This diet went on to form the Kingdom of Rotheran, which had a more centralised version of the Daskau system with a hereditary monarch and a diet of the nobility that voted on the king's decisions. In 1698, Rotheran merged Flanren and Dasseran to form the continental empire of Eurona. This venture only lasted just over a century though, as the Flanrense and Dasseraners began to feel under-represented and peacfully declared independances in 1805 and 1808 respectively.

Following the split up, tensions between Flanren and Rotheran soured significantly. Although in all honesty, it was incredibly one sided, as the Flanrense were simply focusing on trading and building up a modern nation. The Rothers on the other hand were furious at them for abandoning their empire (despite the fact they chose to let the Flanrense go) and enaged in a number of pointless border skirmishes until relations improved through the mid 1800s. Rotheran wouldn't have good relations with Dasseran for much longer however, and did nothing when they were invaded by the Kalernans in 1880. Throughout the 19th century Rotheran had danced with the idea of a republic, but now the monarchy was beginning to centralise power in order to put a halt to it.
