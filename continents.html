Western Continents and large islands

  • Corindum (Cor-in-dum)
  • Coloma (Coh-loh-mah)
  • Sina (See-nah)
  • Altea (Al-tee-ah)
  • Eurona (Ew-roh-nah)

Corindum is the westernmost continent of Tellus, and the birthplace of it's greatest empire, which shares the same name. Corindum is largely split in two by it's central mountain range, with the north-east being largely mediterranean in climate and culture and the south-west being tropical with a strong babylonian culture.

Coloma is a large island directly south-east from Corindum. It shares the tropical climate of southern Corindum while having a more unique culture that remained behind in the deep jungle, hidden from the empire of ages past.

Sina was once the gem of the old empire of Corindum, named for the god of the homesick as it was very similar to the homeland. Sina is more arid inland, but shares a mediteranean style coastline in the western and northern areas. In the south lies a great desert that stretches across the southern coast. The cultures of Sina are tied to those of old Corindum, similar to the romance and north african cultures.

Altea is the most barren land of them all, with the place being so horribly difficult to colonise that it's people still have parts of their original culture. It was named for the Cordindainist god of bitterness and malice. A common joke made of the denizens of Altea is that they are godless, as the original god was forgotten from the Cordindainist pantheon millenia ago.

Eurona has the privilige of being one of the nicest continents to live in. Covered in temperate forests and marshlands, it is very germanic in both culture and landscape. Eurona is also named for a deity, this time being the cordindainist goddess of kindness, although she did over time become known as the goddess of beer.

Eastern Continent and large islands

  • Taijian (Tai-zhi-an)
  • Niton (Ni-ton)

Taijian is the sole continent in the east of Tellus, seperated from the west by the Albe and the Tokinashu Oceans. From the cold mountains of the Krown and Zhanli mountains in the north to the vast plains, criss crossed by rivers in the centre the across the Maisei range unto the grand jungle of the Sarkanin and the savanah of the Solchats.
