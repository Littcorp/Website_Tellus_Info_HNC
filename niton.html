Taijian - Niton

  • Pronunciation: Knee-tohn
  • Capital City: Roteso
  • Languages: Nitonese, Hokeni
  • Cultural Group: Maisei
  • Religion: Pakku, Tacitus Astropathism
  • Government: Military Dictatorship
  • Founded in: 1456

Niton started out as two kingdoms, east and west, Niton and Hokeni. It was always imagined by both sides that they would never unite, both had a fierce streak of independence and isolationism. That was until 1420, and the instatement of Emperor Junsho in Niton. Junsho was radical for his time; expansionist and an outspoken opponent of isolationism, but that was what Niton needed. over the next three deacdes, Junsho slowly occupied more and more of Hokeni until it was entirely under his control. He signed a treaty of peace with not a general or king, but with a farmer; meant to symbolise that the poeple of Hokeni bowed down to him. the next few centuries were spent in slow expansion into the various islands of Taijian. But after a string of peaceful emperors, the military performed a coup and put a Shogun in place.

The shogunate would reign supreme without a challenge to its authority until the 1850s, where a popular movement began growing to put the figurehead emperor back on the throne. This prompted the shogun at the time, Tonokera Daibansu, to introduce a series of massive reforms that brought the country under "Tonokism", also known as communism with imperialist charecteristics. In 1878, Niton, Angal and Kamarov declared the third Taijian war, that would end only four years later. Niton would walk away from the war with vast territories in the Sarkanin and that same year would gain sarenam as a puppet state, although the south broke off in a revolution. This brought much needed resources into Niton and allowed it to properly achive Tonokism.
