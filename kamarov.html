Kamarov was the first of two nations that were created by one man's will, the other being Li'an. Before the 1800s, there was no real concept of a unified Solchat state and especially not one that could be a major power. This was all changed by the 1822 Second Taijian War , which saw the various Solchat duchies unite to face a common enemy. In times of crisis, they elected a noble to the role of Czar, and for the first time in a century, one was elected. Marshal Zhumets Kamarov was chosen following his great victory repelling the Taijians from Torianov. Czar Zhumets would strike north and capture much land over the next three years, only stopped at the Battle of the Titans. On that battlefied, he would meet with the emperor of Taijian and agree that the bloodshed ought to end here and now, and hammered out a treaty.

Zhumets would be given a large chunk of territory, which he named after himself; Kamarov. This new duchy would go on to massively prosper under Zhumets's rule, growing powerful enough to match every other duchy. And that was exactly what Zhumets intended to use it for, declaring a war of unification which went from 1851 to 1860 - a surprsingly short amount of time for a war of such scale. At the end of it he would move his capital to Pyotrsburg and declare himself Czar of the Czardom of Kamarov. In 1878 he led his nation into the third Taijian war, bringing more land under the control of his new empire despite doing almost nothing and letting the Nitonese and Angals do most of the war-winning. Kamarovans tend to be extremely proud of the Third Taijian War.
