Tellus - Home page

Tellus is a worldbuilding project that i have been building for a long time.

Link is here. Tellus is a world larger than our own though it lacks the same density, ergo the gravity is effectively the same. The landmasses are split into the east and west by the Albe and Tokinashu Oceans, the latter of which had been too large to cross until modern steam ships made the voyage. To the north and south lie the Serpentis and the Tacitus, frigid oceans that are rife with icebergs and a hazard year-round.

Of Tellus's landmasses, those that lie in the west are fragmented and split by numerous seas and straits, yet there is a far greater cultural unity here than in the east. The continents here are Corindum, Sina, Altea and Eurona.

Unto the east lies the lone continent of Taijian, known to the west as "Tekar". It stands alone as the onlt continent of the east, despite claims from the Nitonese that they are their own continent. Here lie the most diverse cultures, from the Angals and Eireanns of the cold north, to the Liao and Mai of the great plains and to the Sarkans and Solchats of the tropical south.
