History

Holapsk has had a history full of strife and war. Ever since the days of antiquity, Holapsk has been the staging area of choice for any eager Corin empire and the easiest target to raid by pirates and corsairs. Despite these constant challenges, the great king Sadislaw united the various tribes of the holapski in the 1300s and created a kingdom that would last for some centuries. Holapsk continued as they were until the 1680s, and an offer from the so-called Sinan Empire. The offer was that of joining a union that was similar to that of Rotheran's system, which promised the nobles some power over the emperor. They accepted after a long period of consideration.

As the years went however, the emperors centralised power more and more, eventually evolving into an absolutist state. This came to a head in 1852 with the populace staging a general uprising and establishing a republic, but it would only last until 1870. That year, Kalerno invaded and began an occupation. The populous began a fierce policy of both civil disobediance and outright partisan warfare, partisans that quickly gained a reputation for taking no prisoners - it wasn't like they could if they wanted to. The wide open plains of Holapsk became known as the wild west, as outlaws and criminals roamed freely thanks to the fighting. Notably, the Holapski partisans invented the machine gun to better kill the occupiers. By the time of the great war and Holapsk's liberation by Matreaux, the partisans held a good portion of territory on their own.
