    • History

    Laurel is a continent-spanning nation that completely covers Corindum. Founded in its modern form in 1762, Laurel was born out of a centuries-long series of conquests that ended in the "Second Bush War" that spanned from 1722 until the proper founding of Laurel. The Bush War lasted so long due to the slow pace of the conflict and most of the battles being smaller skirmishes, but by the 1760s the last of their enemies, Cantuk, had fallen. While the first king of Laurel, the one who won the Bush War, was consumed by a mad desire to be a true emperor, his son was quick to undo much of the absolutist laws that were put in place. Instead, a senate was set up in order to share the power in an attempt to establish a longevity.
