History

Laurel is a continent-spanning nation that completely covers Corindum. Founded in its modern form in 1762, Laurel was born out of a centuries-long series of conquests that ended in the "Second Bush War" that spanned from 1722 until the proper founding of Laurel. The Bush War lasted so long due to the slow pace of the conflict and most of the battles being smaller skirmishes, but by the 1760s the last of their enemies, Cantuk, had fallen. While the first king of Laurel, the one who won the Bush War, was consumed by a mad desire to be a true emperor, his son was quick to undo much of the absolutist laws that were put in place. Instead, a senate was set up in order to share the power in an attempt to establish a longevity.

Laurel would soon enter a period of stagnantion, with a series of incredibly isolationist policies that only Niton were allowed to be exmept from. This was due to the Nitonese crossing the Tokinashu ocean (the man who organised it was named Tokinashu, the ocean named for him) which greatly impressed the king and senate, which allowed for some limited industrialisation. As time went on, Laurel would begin to have political forces push for the country to open and equally loud voices opposing them. The anti-isolation groups were largely conservative, monarchist and strangely, communist. Opposing them was initially the centrists and liberals and later, by 1920, far right extremists began to appear on their side, most importantly the League of the Eagle.
