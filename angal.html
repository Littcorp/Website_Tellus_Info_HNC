History

Angal is a relatively small nation situated at the very north of Taijian, yet it is arguably its strongest power. While the Angal of modern times was founded in 1842, it had existed for over a millenia beforehand. During this early period, Angal was a backwater without any major wealth and entirely reliant on subsistence farming. This came to an abrupt end in 1795 after the discovery of vast resource deposits under the Krown Range and caused the superpower of the time, the Taijian Dynasty, to invade. While the Dynasty won quickly, the Angals fought a long war of resistance that led to them regaining their independance in 1842. What followed was a couple of years of terror and brutal repression of those who were seen as collaborators before king Sin-Kai Eldwin officially pardoned everyone who had worked with the Dynasty.

What followed was an explosion of industrialisation that saw Angal become one of the most economically powerful nations on the planet. The sudden and vast expansion of everything, from government to agriculture to military, gave Eldwin an idea. In the dead of night one summer's day in 1863, Angal launched a surprise invasion into Eire. While initially successful in their capture of the nearest large island; Cordane, Angal would quickly stall into a frustrating stalemate. The war would end with Angal permanently gaining Cordane but having to give up some islands in the Straits of Angal. Barely more than a decade later, Angal would play its role in the Third Taijian War and retake the last part of their lands under foreign occupation.
