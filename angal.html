  • Capital City: Yan-Ko

  • Cultural Group: Serpentis
  • Founded in: 1842

Angal is the current world superpower, its position as such only challenged by its long-time rival Niton and the rapidly ascendant Corindum. It emerged onto the world stage after fifty years of occupation in 1842 and ever since has been on a meteoric rise, winning its position of superiority through the Great War, Norderkrieg and the Central Taijian War. It is a strange place when compared to the rest of the world, with an archaic economic system and absolutist monarchy that are in a sharp contrast with Angal’s vast rights for its citizens and a cultural gender equality. While not often talked about by many within Angal, they have much blood on their hands that many outside of the country remember bitterly.
