Major Religions of Tellus

  1. Pakku (Pak-ku)
  2. Cordindainism (Cor-din-dayn-ism)
  3. Astropathism (As-tro-path-ism)
  4. Chalgoism (Kal-go-ism)
  5. Unism (Yoon-ism)

Pakku is the largest religion in all of Tellus, largely since it was part of the Liandi conquests and the culture they spread. It is polytheistic in nature and revolves around a pantheon of deities that manage the "heavenly Bureaucracy" which is how the world gets rain and days, for exmaple. When there is a drought, it is said that some gods are feuding and blocking a portion of the bureaucratic pipeline. In their version of creation, it is said that Tellus came to be over a feud in which the gods had to settle a bet over who could create the most work for another to do.

Cordindainism is the largest religion in the western world, with it being majority in every western continent other than Eurona. It is another polytheistic religion, going back all the way to the old empire of Corindum.
