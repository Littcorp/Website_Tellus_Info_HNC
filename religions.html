Major Religions of Tellus

  1. Pakku (Pak-ku)
  2. Cordindainism (Cor-din-dayn-ism)
  3. Astropathism (As-tro-path-ism)
  4. Chalgoism (Kal-go-ism)
  5. Unism (Yoon-ism)

Pakku is the largest religion in all of Tellus, largely since it was part of the Liandi conquests and the culture they spread. It is polytheistic in nature and revolves around a pantheon of deities that manage the "heavenly Bureaucracy" which is how the world gets rain and days, for exmaple. When there is a drought, it is said that some gods are feuding and blocking a portion of the bureaucratic pipeline. In their version of creation, it is said that Tellus came to be over a feud in which the gods had to settle a bet over who could create the most work for another to do.

Cordindainism is the largest religion in the western world, with it being majority in every western continent other than Eurona. It is another polytheistic religion, going back all the way to the old empire of Corindum which spread it in much the same way as the Liandi spread Pakku around Taijian. It revolves around a Greek/Roman style pantheon of deities and many of the stories and such are similar. In the Cordindainist idea of creation, the world has just always been there, as with the gods. The gods just kept fucking up how to get free will to work without evil, so they gave up and just made an afterlife for bad people to suffer in, although that is an oversimplification.

Astropathism is the oldest religion on Tellus, with archeological evidence pointing to it being at least ten thousand years old. It is split into two main denominations; Tacitus in the south and Serpentis in the north, Tacitus Astropathism is focused on worship on the sun "Soluaa" while Serpentis Astropathism puts primary worship on the north star "Poluraa" with the sun being a close second. Both denominations agree on that the moon is right below the sun in worship.
